What is drawn

From the run: every boundary point of each swimmer, the fluid velocity (stored on a 2-cell grid, smoothed by a cubic B-spline here), the wall and post surfaces. Tracers are massless points integrated through that velocity in the browser; each trail joins its positions once per beat over the last 8 beats, so it shows net drift, not the oscillation within a beat.

Drawing choices: the head outline is the ring of points grown by 0.6 cell, the tail ribbon tapers from 2.3 to 0.7 cells wide, and positions and flow are interpolated between 8 records per beat. The method spreads each point force over 4 cells, so on the fluid a body acts about half a cell fatter than its boundary points.

These are prescribed-gait bodies, not evolved agents. The fluid starts at rest; there is no background flow and no chemical signal. The box is periodic left to right.
