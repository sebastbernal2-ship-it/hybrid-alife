# PPO-POET held-out replication campaign

This archive contains two independent poet-versus-static replications.

## Design

- Tracks: `poet` and `static`.
- Seeds: `0` through `9` per replication.
- Generations: `200` per cell.
- Held-out environments: `4` per cell.
- Transfer score: undiscounted raw reward from a deterministic frozen-policy rollout.
- Source commit: `eaf16dbfe41c981c822e6778a98126121efb5d04`.
- Artifact schema: `2`.

The `static` track keeps the environment population fixed while training the paired policy population.
The `poet` track admits mutated environments after the declared raw-reward criterion.
Held-out environments are generated from a separate seed stream and excluded from training and admission.

## Preregistered gate

The analyzer found positive pooled effect, but the preregistered success gate was not met.
The correct conclusion is `insufficient_evidence`.

| Replication | Mean poet - static | Exact paired p | 10 pairs |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `replication-1` | 0.1875 | 0.73828125 | yes |
| `replication-2` | 0.8726 | 0.109375 | yes |
| pooled | 0.5301 | not used for gate | 95% bootstrap CI [-0.1593, 1.2303] |

The pooled confidence interval crosses zero.
Neither replication has exact paired `p < 0.05`.
This archive does not support a preregistered success claim.
