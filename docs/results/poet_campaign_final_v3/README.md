# PPO-POET corrected campaign archive

This directory contains the corrected 30-cell PPO-POET campaign.

## Campaign design

- Tracks: `isolated`, `avida_enabled`, and `avida_persistent`.
- Seeds: `0` through `9` per track.
- Generations: `200` per cell.
- World size: `8` by `8`.
- Population size: `2` policies and `2` environments.
- Rollout horizon: `8` steps.
- Transfer horizon: `8` steps.
- Device: CPU.
- Transfer score: undiscounted sum of per-step rewards from a deterministic frozen-policy rollout.

The original 20-cell design is rerun with the corrected transfer metric.
The persistent Avida lifecycle adds 10 cells.

## Avida lifecycle tracks

- `avida_enabled` resets the Avida population for each rollout.
- `avida_persistent` carries each environment slot's Avida population across PPO training rollouts and generations.
- Both tracks are bounded comparisons and do not establish open-ended evolution.

## Results

The corrected final transfer means are:

| Track | Mean | Standard deviation |
|---|---:|---:|
| `isolated` | 8.5725 | 2.5673 |
| `avida_enabled` | 9.1884 | 2.5968 |
| `avida_persistent` | 8.6865 | 2.7859 |

Paired comparisons use matching seeds and exact sign-permutation tests:

| Comparison | Mean difference | Wins | Cliff's delta | Exact p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `avida_enabled - isolated` | 0.6159 | 9/10 | 0.24 | 0.3262 |
| `avida_persistent - isolated` | 0.1141 | 7/10 | 0.12 | 0.8789 |
| `avida_persistent - avida_enabled` | -0.5018 | 6/10 | -0.04 | 0.6504 |

These results are descriptive.
They do not establish an Avida advantage.
